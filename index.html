Energy management. Home security. Outdoor entertainment. These are just a few of our favorite things. Or put everything together in a home automation system. With an updated wireless network setup and Control4 programming, you can enjoy one-touch control – the lights dim, the music turns on, the heat turns on, the shades drop and the doors lock. Leaving you with nothing to do but enjoy your home with the people that matter.

Based in Atlanta, GA, we treat our customers with southern hospitality. We work closely with our builders, designers, remodelers and vendors to install smart home wiring and create dream homes that provide efficiency, comfort, security and state-of-the-art audio and visual entertainment.
