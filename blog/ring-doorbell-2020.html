Now that we’re a few months into 2021, it’s a great time to look back at the smart home devices that defined the past year. In 2020, video doorbells became more popular than ever, and Ring continued to lead the pack with a lineup that catered to just about every type of user.

So, which Ring doorbell actually stood out as the best in 2020? Here’s a look back.

Why Ring Dominated in 2020

Throughout 2020, Ring maintained its position as one of the most recognizable names in home security. With more people staying home and investing in smart devices, the demand for reliable, easy-to-use video doorbells surged.

Ring’s strength was simple:

      • User-friendly setup
      • Solid mobile app integration
      • A wide range of models at different price points

Whether you were new to smart home tech or upgrading an older device, Ring had something that fit.

Best Overall: Ring Video Doorbell 3

Looking back, the Ring Video Doorbell 3 clearly stood out as the best all-around choice in 2020.

Why it earned that spot:

      • Enhanced Motion Detection: More control over motion zones helped reduce unnecessary alerts.
      • Dual-Band Wi-Fi: Support for both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks improved connectivity and performance.
      • Quick-Release Battery: Charging was faster and more convenient compared to earlier models.
      • 1080p HD Video: Delivered clear video quality day and night.

At the time, it struck a near-perfect balance between features, performance, and price—making it the go-to recommendation for most households.

Best Budget Pick: Ring Video Doorbell (2nd Generation)

For those who didn’t need all the bells and whistles, the Ring Video Doorbell (2nd Gen) was a standout value in 2020.

It offered the essentials:

      • 1080p HD video
      • Motion alerts
      • Two-way talk
      • Competitive pricing

Even now in early 2021, it still holds up as a reliable entry-level option for basic home monitoring.

Ring Video Doorbell (2nd Generation)

Best Premium Option: Ring Video Doorbell Pro

If you wanted a more refined, permanently installed solution, the Ring Video Doorbell Pro was the premium pick in 2020.

What made it appealing:

      • Sleek, slim design
      • Customizable motion zones
      • Hardwired for constant power
      • Faster response times

It was especially popular among homeowners looking for a more integrated, always-on experience.

Subscription Considerations

One thing that stood out in 2020—and still applies in 2021—is Ring’s subscription model. The Ring Protect Plan unlocks video recording, history, and sharing features.

Without it, you’re limited to live view and alerts. For many users, the subscription became part of the overall cost of owning a Ring device.

Final Verdict (From a 2021 Perspective)

Looking back from March 2021, the Ring Video Doorbell 3 remains the best Ring doorbell of 2020. It delivered the strongest combination of features and usability without pushing into premium pricing.

That said:

      • Choose the 2nd Gen if you want something affordable and reliable
      • Go with the Doorbell 3 for the best balance
      • Pick the Pro if you prefer a sleek, hardwired setup

2020 was a turning point. With more people prioritizing home security and convenience, video doorbells went from a “nice-to-have” gadget to a common household feature.

As we move further into 2021, newer models are already entering the market—but the devices from 2020 laid the groundwork for what smart home security looks like today..
